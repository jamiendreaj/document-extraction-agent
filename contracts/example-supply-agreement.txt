MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made between Verano Foods S.p.A., a company incorporated in
Italy with registered office at Via dei Mulini 14, 10024 Moncalieri (TO)
("the Customer"), and Northbrook Analytics Ltd, a company incorporated in
England and Wales with registered office at 3 Pellow Yard, Bristol BS1 4QA
("the Supplier").

The parties signed this Agreement on 14 January 2027.

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Effective Date" means 1 February 2027.

1.2 "Fees" means the charges set out in Schedule 1.

1.3 "Services" means the data analysis services described in Schedule 1.

2. TERM

2.1 This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and shall continue for an
initial term of twenty-four (24) months (the "Initial Term").

2.2 At the end of the Initial Term this Agreement shall automatically renew for
successive periods of twelve (12) months each, unless either party gives notice
of non-renewal in accordance with clause 11.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 The Customer shall pay the Fees within forty-five (45) days of the date of
each valid invoice.

3.2 Interest on late payment shall accrue at four per cent (4%) per annum above
the base rate.

9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

9.1 Nothing in this clause limits either party's liability for death or personal
injury caused by negligence, or for fraud.

9.2 Subject to clause 9.1, each party's total aggregate liability arising out of
or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the aggregate Fees paid by
the Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the
claim.

11. NOTICES AND NON-RENEWAL

11.1 A party wishing to prevent renewal under clause 2.2 shall give the other
party not less than thirty (30) days written notice before the end of the then
current term.

11.2 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand
or sent by recorded post to the address given above.

14. GOVERNING LAW

14.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising out of it shall be governed by and
construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

SCHEDULE 1 - THE SERVICES AND FEES

The Supplier shall provide monthly reporting and a quarterly review. The Fees
are EUR 4.500,00 per month, invoiced monthly in arrears.

SCHEDULE 2 - OPERATIONAL PROVISIONS

Non-renewal. Notwithstanding anything else in this Agreement, a party wishing to
prevent renewal shall give not less than sixty (60) days written notice before
the end of the then current term.

Escalation. Service issues shall be escalated to the account manager within two
(2) working days.
